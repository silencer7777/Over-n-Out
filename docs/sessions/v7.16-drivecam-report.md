# V7.16 — Driving chase camera (playtest fix: "fix the driving trucks — you can't see")

Brett's words (Nov 4, ~17:03 PT, after playing V7.15 live):
"fix the driving trucks you can't see."

## Diagnosis (proven, not guessed)

Three candidate defects were on the table — trucks invisible in
the world, the driver's view blocked by the truck itself, trucks
invisible online — and the answer is the second one, with a
precise mechanism:

- The truck model itself is fine: 1,584 verts, no NaNs, same
  merged-geometry pipeline as the (visible) bots; a bystander
  raycast hits it normally; online uses the identical actor.
- **The driver's eye sat INSIDE the truck cab** at rig-local
  (0, seatHeight 1.55, 0), and the truck's opaque windshield box
  sits 0.84 m ahead at exactly eye height (y 1.35–1.93). A
  raycast grid from that eye (real vendored three, real actor;
  raycast culling probe-verified to match the rasterizer)
  showed **~66% of the forward view filled with vehicle surface
  under 2 m away** — the windshield slab dead ahead, hood below
  it. The driver was staring at the back of his own windshield;
  the road ahead was BEHIND that glass, invisible. The quad's
  open-air eye was clear (control case), which is why this read
  as a *truck* problem.

## The fix

- **Chase camera for driving** (solo + online share one pose,
  `poseDriveRig` in main.js): the camera rides behind/above
  the vehicle from new spec fields (`camBack`/`camLift` in
  js/vehicles.js — truck 6.2 m back / 1.9 m lift, quad 4.8 /
  1.5), tilted down by a spec-derived angle so the vehicle
  sits low-centre in frame and the road reads over it. Mouse
  pitch still glances up/down at half gain.
- The desired camera point is run through the game's own
  `collide()` (0.35 m radius, vehicle-feet filtering) so
  backing against a wall pulls the camera in instead of
  burying it inside the wall; a ground guard keeps it off the
  dirt on slopes.
- Both exit paths (solo `exitVehicle`, the online transition)
  and `Player.reset()` restore the on-foot eye
  (`camera.position.set(0, EYE, 0)`) — the chase offset leaves
  x/z non-zero and nothing else rewrites them.
- Vehicle physics, prediction, run-over, armor split, FOV
  bloom, engine loop: untouched.

## Proof

New permanent gate **tools/drivecam-check.mjs** (Item 10
promotion — the diagnosis probes became a gate, wired into
tools/gates-parallel.sh): DC1 geometry sane; DC2 defect
witness (old eye's level ray hits the windshield at 0.80 m;
quad control clear); DC3 chase view (view-axis ray meets the
truck at 6.26 m / quad 5.32 m; view over the vehicle clear
for 25 m; truck fills 10% of the view grid vs the old ~66%);
DC4 wiring pins. Smoke gains **VH8** (spec offsets + shared
pose + both eye restores).

## Known cosmetic gap (staged, honestly)

There is no local-player body model in this game (first-person
rig is camera + gun), so in chase view the truck drives with an
empty seat. A seated driver figure is real rigging work; it
belongs with the character pass, not this fix. Brett should
judge the camera itself: you can see the truck, and you can
see the road.

hood line, and the view should stay clean when you back
