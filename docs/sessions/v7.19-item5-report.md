## 5a) Trip wires — already in the game; now PROVEN

The TRIP MINE is the game's tripwire and it was already fully
wired before this slice: selectable in the lethal slot
(loadouts LETHALS), placed with the standard deploy flow, beam
rendered as a thin red line (opacity 0.35 — spot it on close
inspection; 0.85 with the Engineer perk), bots and players trip
it, and the authoritative sim evaluates crossings with the same
shared math (js/equipment-data.js). What was missing was any
permanent proof. New gate **tools/tripwire-check.mjs**:

- TW1 spec: beam 0.6 m high, up to 4 m long, cross tolerance
  0.4 m, jump-clear at feet > 0.75 m, 0.25 s detonation delay
  (a sprinter can outrun the worst of it), 1 per life, 20 hp
  (shootable), 8 m spawn-exclusion placement law.
- TW2 geometry: crossing the beam trips (segment distance 0);
  passing 1 m beside it or 1 m past its end does not.
- TW3 damage: radial from the device — 100 inside 1.5 m, 65 at
  2.75 m, 30 at the 4 m edge, 0 beyond. Players take ×0.45
  through the damage path (max 45 — hurts, never insta-kills).
  Bots take full damage. Conservative numbers, unchanged.
- TW4 wiring: the jump-clear crossing rule + delay exist in
  BOTH solo (main.js) and the sim (server-validated — no
  client-trusted explosions).
- TW5 synergy: the V7.18 chokepoints (bridge, passage, depot
  door) are all legal placements — nearest spawn 23.3 m away.

Balance: NO numbers changed. Brett's ask was capability; the
capability existed — the gap was verification + discoverability
(the LOADOUT lethal row names it; the blurb explains counterplay:
"jump it, sprint it, or shoot the mine").

## 5b) Underground passages — covered passage shipped; true tunnels specced

Shipped in V7.18: the South Service Passage (covered corridor,
two entrances, interior cover, proven lanes in bridge-check
BC3) — the enclosed flanking route gameplay, at grade. True
below-grade tunnels remain BLOCKED on the ground contract:
`groundHeightAt` returns the highest standable top within a
step of your feet; it cannot express "floor at −2.5 under a
slab whose top is the street" without a layered-floor contract
change across js/world.js, all four data modules, and net/sim.
That change is the spec: add a `floorsAt(x, z)` list contract,
keep `groundHeightAt` as its step-filtered view, extend
reachability-maps' floorsAt proof (it already computes layered
floors) to the compound. Minimap decision stands: underground
routes FADE when built (hidden movement, dimmed dot).

## 5c) Clan wars — groundwork shipped, war records specced (nothing faked)

Shipped (all client-profile/account-independent):
- **js/clans.js** — one sanitizer shared by client + sim +
  checks: tag 2–5 chars uppercase alnum, name ≤24 de-bracketed.
- **Profile**: `meta.clan {name, tag}` in the versioned profile
  (createProfile + sanitizeProfile, repaired like everything
  else). RECORD tab has the clan name/tag inputs.
- **Display**: the tag prefixes the callsign ([TAG] Name,
  inside the existing 24-char cap) everywhere names flow —
  menus, chat, scoreboard, killfeed.
- **Online**: hello carries a structured `clan` field;
  net/protocol.js shape-checks it (≤5 alnum, garbage refused at
  the handshake); net/server.js threads it; the sim stores
  `clanTag` on the player record (sanitized server-side). That
  record field is the seam team-assignment will key on.
- **PLAY tab**: a ⚔ CLAN WAR entry point that tells the truth —
  set a tag now; wars unlock with accounts.
- Smoke CL1–CL4 pin all of it.

NOT shipped, by design (the Stage 2 doc §9 has the full spec):
clan registry with tag ownership, membership/invites, war
records, and clan-vs-clan team assignment. The blocking fact is
architectural, stated plainly: there are no accounts (identity
is a per-connection callsign anyone can type), and the sim
fields ONE human team ('you') against Kestrel bots — a second
human team needs bot-fill/win-condition work in the sim, not a
flag. §9 maps Brett's three open Stage 2 decisions: Q1 (guests)
decides whether guests may carry tags into war rooms; Q2
(callsign changes) does NOT block (membership keys on account
id); Q3 (invite code) does not block but its mechanism is the
natural clan-invite transport. The real gate is the Stage 2
build itself.
