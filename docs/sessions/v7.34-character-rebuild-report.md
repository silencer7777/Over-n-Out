## Root cause found (the rebuild's premise)

Prior de-blockify passes added rounded overlays ON the
hit-core boxes, but many overlays are SMALLER than the
cores: the shin cylinder (r 0.080–0.100), ankle taper
(r 0.068–0.082) and boot collar sat **fully inside** the
leg core (half-extents 0.105 × 0.125) and could never
show; the thigh cylinder, ribcage and waist sash left the
cores' flat faces and vertical corners exposed; the face
sphere (r 0.168) sat inside the head core's half-width
(0.17). The flat boxes still defined the silhouette.
Online puppets were raw boxes throughout.
