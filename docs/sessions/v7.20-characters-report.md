**Where they live:**
- **LOADOUT tab** — an OPERATOR picker (all 15, with role +
  outfit blurb). The choice persists in the profile
  (sanitize-repaired like everything else; unknown ids fall
  back to REYES).
- **Solo bots** rotate the full roster as they spawn — squads
  stop being clones; each bot IS an operator (deterministic
  outfit over the standard wear jitter).
- **Online**: the choice rides the hello (`character`,
  shape-checked; the sim stores only real roster ids), the
  snapshot carries it per player AND per bot (sim bots rotate
  the roster deterministically from the spawn ordinal — zero
  rng-stream cost), and remote puppets re-skin from it
  (uniform + kit + skin; visor stays faction — the readability
  law). Your operator is who everyone else sees.

**Human-like base**: the roster stands on the shipped soldier
work — V7.6 completed kit (chin straps, headset pair, dump
pouch, hydration tube), rounded anatomy (tapered limbs, joint
spheres, jaw/nose/ears face read), five skin tones. This slice
adds identity variety on that base, not a second rig.

## The honest constraint (and why it is also the win)

Identity is palette-only BY LAW in this engine: the metrics
CAMO VETO pins any palette swap at **Δ0 draw calls / Δ0
triangles**, and characters.js exports no geometry at all
(gate CH2). Measured after this slice: **8 calls / 6,104 tris
per soldier, palette delta 0/0** — a match full of 15
different operators costs exactly what a match of clones
costs. That is how "up to 15" fits a phone. What palette-only
cannot do: different BODY GEOMETRY per operator (builds,
headgear shapes). The hit cores are byte-identical V3 volumes
for every soldier who has ever shipped — changing silhouettes
per character would fork the hitbox contract and the cost
audit; it is recorded here as a deliberate non-goal for this
slice, not an oversight. All operators share one hitbox class
and one stat line — visual identity only, no exceptions.
