## What the old bullets were, in code terms

A `THREE.Line` (two points, LineBasicMaterial) drawn INSTANTLY
along the ENTIRE muzzle-to-impact path, held for 0.05 s, then
faded. Why it read fake:

- **No travel.** The round teleported: the full path lit up in
  the same frame at 5 m and at 100 m. Nothing moved, so there
  was no sense of a projectile at all — just a connection
  between gun and target.
- **Laser geometry.** One pixel wide (WebGL ignores linewidth),
  flat 0.9 opacity, pastel color, no glow behavior: a needle /
  laser beam, not a hot tracer core.
- **No speed identity.** Every weapon flashed identically even
  though the weapon table already publishes a per-weapon
  muzzle velocity (AR 880, DMR 960, SMG 420, shotgun 390,
  pistol 375) — the number existed and the render ignored it.

## The new visual — a traveling streak (chosen over meshes)

Route chosen: keep the existing pooled Line slots (32, zero
new objects, zero new draw calls) and make the segment a
SHORT STREAK THAT TRAVELS:

- Streak length = 22% of the flight path, clamped 1.6–8
  units — a bright core with a short tail, never the whole
  path at once.
- The head advances at the weapon's table `velocity`; the
  tail follows one streak-length behind and collapses into
  the impact point; total life = (dist + len) / velocity,
  floored at 35 ms (point-blank still flashes) and capped at
  0.5 s. Worked examples: AR at 60 m — 8-unit streak, ~77 ms
  of visible travel; DMR at 110 m — ~123 ms streak across the
  map; pistol at 20 m — ~65 ms, slower and stubbier, as a
  pistol round should read.
- Additive blending + strike-in/sustain/fade-out opacity
  envelope, so the core reads hot against bright sky and dark
  ground alike.
- One shared `stepTracers(dt)` now advances streaks from both
  the offline and the online tick (remote shots in online
  matches render with the shooter's weapon velocity too).
- Unchanged invariants: pool size 32, pellet tracer cap 3,
  suppressed weapons still render NO tracer (stealth
  identity), prestige tracer tints still set the color,
  muzzle-flash behavior untouched, no shadow casters added
  (lines cast none), frustumCulled still false.
