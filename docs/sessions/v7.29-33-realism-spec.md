## 1. THE REALISM CHECK (defined before building, per directive)

Evidence: rendered screenshots from the LIVE-equivalent local
build via tools/realism-shots.mjs (headless Chromium, gameplay
camera height ~1.6 m, HUD-minimal world views), saved under
docs/sessions/realism-shots/<version>/. Character frames: bot at
~4 m (close), ~12 m (mid), ~30 m (far), mid-stride vs standing.
Scenery frames: one wide + one lane view per map.

Scoring per criterion: PASS / MARGINAL / FAIL with the frame as
evidence. "Some level of realistic" (the pass bar) = every
character criterion ≥ MARGINAL with C1 and C2 at PASS, and every
scenery criterion ≥ MARGINAL on at least 4 of 5 maps with no
FAIL on the fifth. Anything below that = keep building slices.
Full CoD parity is explicitly NOT the bar (AAA mocap/PBR vs a
procedural browser build); the benchmark is the direction and
the measuring stick, scored honestly.

### Character criteria (benchmark: CoD core-MP operator frames)
- C1 WALK READ: at mid distance, a moving soldier's legs visibly
  alternate stride (two frames 0.25 s apart show opposite leg
  phases); the figure no longer reads as gliding/statue-stiff.
- C2 SILHOUETTE: at close distance the outline reads as a person
  in kit — no uninterrupted crate-flat planes on legs/torso; hip
  and knee breaks visible in the profile.
- C3 PROPORTIONS: head ~1/7.5 of height, shoulders wider than
  hips, legs ~half of height (measure on the close frame).
- C4 WEAPON CARRY: gun sits in both hands at shoulder line, arms
  reach it plausibly, no floating/intersecting weapon.
- C5 STANCE: idle/aim stance is a believable ready posture (not
  T-pose, not mannequin symmetry) at close distance.
- C6 DISTANCE READ: at far distance a moving soldier still reads
  as a moving person (gait or bob cadence visible), faction still
  readable by visor/rig colour (standing readability law).

### Scenery criteria (benchmark matched per map class)
Compound = CoD core-MP small map. Forest/Snow/Mars/Orbital =
Ground War / Warzone environment frames (scale, depth, dressing).
- S1 DEPTH: background layers outside the playable bounds
  (ridges/structures/skyline) read at ≥3 depth planes in the wide
  frame; no void horizon.
- S2 GROUND: ground is not a flat colour field at lane distance —
  texture variation, cover scatter or wear breaks it up.
- S3 DRESSING DENSITY: lane frame shows believable set dressing
  (cover, props, vegetation/structures) at CoD-lane density
  class — no long empty corridors of bare ground/wall.
- S4 ELEVATION: playable verticality visible in frame (raised
  positions, ramps, multi-level structures) where the map claims
  it.
- S5 LIGHT/SHADOW FEEL: figures and cover sit IN the scene
  (contact grounding, cast shadows present), no floating or
  pasted-on look. (Grade itself is owner-locked; this scores
  grounding only.)
- S6 SKY/ATMOSPHERE: sky reads as atmosphere (cloud layer,
  gradient, celestial body as applicable), not a flat backdrop.

### Shooting-feel criteria (owner order 1; judged in-session)
- F1 RECOIL: sustained auto fire climbs in a learnable pattern
  with partial recovery; single shots (DMR) fully recover before
  the next shot can fire; no change to damage/fire-rate table.
- F2 IMPACT: world hits show a visible impact effect at the hit
  point; bot hits give feedback beyond the hitmarker alone.
- F3 TRACER/MUZZLE: tracers read as travelling rounds (V7.12
  streaks — verify, don't regress); muzzle flash present and
  brief for player AND bot fire.
