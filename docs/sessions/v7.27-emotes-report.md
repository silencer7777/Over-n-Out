## Controls (mobile-safe, per the law)

- **B** opens the quick bar (bottom-centre); **1–6** fire a gesture
  (the bar's digits take precedence over weapon slots while it's
  open; it hides on selection or after 4 s).
- Touch: a dedicated **EMOTE** button (left edge, below USE) opens
  the same bar; the slots are big tap targets. `mobile-check`
  passes with the new control in place.
- 1.2 s personal cooldown — emotes are punctuation, not spam.

## Online (authority-safe by construction)

- New protocol type `emote` (MSG 10 → 11, version stays 1; N1
  re-pinned by name). `validateEmote` checks shape only (protocol
  stays dependency-free); the **server** owns membership against
  `js/emotes.js` (`bad_emote` + violation recorded otherwise),
  stamps `from`/`name` from its own record like chat, rate-limits
  in a separate bucket, and relays to the room.
- Clients render the callout line in the chat feed and play the
  puppet gesture in `online.js` — root motion composed so the
  interpolation lerp is never contaminated (offsets are undone
  before each lerp, re-applied after).
