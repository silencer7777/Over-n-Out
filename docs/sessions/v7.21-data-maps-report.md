# V7.21 — Forest / Snow / Mars data expansion (Item 4, second slice) + team upgrades

## Per-map: 124 m → 168 m across (BOUNDS 62 → 84, +35% linear, +83% area)

- **Forest** — treelines move out to ±78..82 with two new ring
  stands; trees 110 → 150, bushes 90 → 120 (density kept, not
  diluted); spawns pushed to the new corners/edges; NEW: the
  **SE Knoll** — a second terraced high ground (0.575 steps to
  2.3) with a watch shelter, mirroring the NW knoll so the map
  has two sniper terraces at opposite corners.
- **Snow Station** — scatter field widened, drifts 46 → 62;
  NEW: the **East Drift Ridge** (three terraces climbing east)
  and a **West Shelter** (posts + snow roof) on the west lane;
  spawns out to ±76/78.
- **Mars Uplink** — rock scatter square ±58 → ±80, rocks
  40 → 54; NEW: a second **solar array** diagonal in the NE
  ring and **regolith terraces** in the SW; spawns out.

Objectives stay in the core by design — the ring is maneuver
space and landmarks; crossing times grow, vehicles and the
spread spawns are the answer (no speed changes, as before).
