# V7.18 — Second-ring expansion: bigger world, water + bridge, a rooftop warehouse, a statue (Items 4/6/7/8, first slice)

Owner directives (Nov 4, ~17:04–17:11 PT): "Expand all maps to
realistic compounds and sizes"; "add some water and bridges";
"add elevation — I want to be able to go on rooftops — and fix
the buildings"; "add more props — vines, statues"; scale

## What players get (Compound — the world under every biome)

The playable square grows **160 m → 208 m across (+30% linear,
+69% area)**: bounds ±80 → ±104. The old ±80 wall stands ruined
in place as a second ruin line with road gaps. The new ring holds:

- **North Gate Plaza + the Gate Warden statue** — a stone sentry
  on a plinth between two gate pillars, with sandbag rows: a
  landmark you can call out ("at the statue") and a new
  transmission pad beside it.
- **East Depot** — a warehouse you can fight inside (door +
  loading-bay gaps, cover crates) AND on top of: exterior stair
  to a parapet roof with HVAC/vent/crate cover, plus a second
  way up — a crate-hop on the east wall (1.15 → 2.3 stack, jump
  to the plate). Glass window bands and vines dress the walls.
- **West Reservoir + causeway bridge** — an 18×44 m water basin
  (animated surface, drifting texture) crossed on the west road
  by a railed bridge: ramps 0 → 0.5 → deck 1.0 → down. **Water
  interaction model: shallow wading** — the basin floor is normal
  ground under ankle-deep visuals; no swim state, no speed
  change (the bot/netcode-safe choice; stated, not silent).
- **South Service Passage** — a covered corridor (walls + roof,
  cover crates inside): the hidden-route/tripwire habitat.
  HONEST SCOPE: true below-grade tunnels are NOT in this slice —
  the game's ground contract (`groundHeightAt` = highest top
  within a step) cannot express a floor below 0 or a ceiling you
  walk under as a separate layer without a contract change
  across world.js + all data modules + the sim. The passage
  delivers the gameplay (enclosed flanking route) at grade; the
  tunnel contract change is specced as its own future slice.
  Minimap decision: at-grade passage shows normally; when true
  tunnels land, underground should FADE on the minimap (you're
  hidden, your dot dims) — recorded here as the standing call.
- **Ring dressing** — jersey barriers and crates on the ring
  roads, drums, sandbags at the depot door, 4 ring spawns.

Fall behaviour: checked — the game has NO fall-damage model
(landing produces a camera dip only). None was added: no silent
balance change. Roof drops are free, as before.
