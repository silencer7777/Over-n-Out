# V7.28 — Movement: step-down snap (Job 2 §8) — slice report

**Status: shipped; live verification in the run log.**

## The change

One surgical change in `js/player.js`: **step-down snapping**.
When a grounded player walks off a drop of **≤ 0.6 m** (the same
height a grounded step can climb) and is not jumping or already
falling, the walker now takes the step down in stride — feet on
the new floor, still grounded — instead of going airborne.

Why this one: Job 2 §3 filled the maps with stairs (the relay
tower, the habitat skirts, the Orbital gantry and porch — every
one built from 0.57 rises). Walking DOWN those stairs was a
staircase of micro-falls: airborne frames, a gravity dip per
step, and landing-dip camera kicks on a hard landing. Descents
now walk like ground. Everything else about movement was
already mature (coyote time, jump buffering, variable jump
height, landing dip, slide, the U7-03 soft-start) and is
untouched.

## What did NOT change (the no-regression evidence)

- No physics constant moved: walk 5.6 / sprint 9.2, GRAVITY 24,
  JUMP_VELOCITY 9.5 (apex ~1.88 m), accel rates, slide numbers —
  all identical. The reachability gates (which pin the jump
  apex) and mobile-check's T16 look proofs pass unmodified.
- A real edge still falls: drops > 0.6 m go airborne exactly as
  before, landing dip included.
- Nothing new becomes reachable: snapping only smooths a
  descent you could always make by falling. Ascent rules are
  byte-identical (proven by SD3 + the §2 zone gate's BFS, which
  models step-up only and still passes everywhere).

## Proof — new permanent gate `tools/stepdown-check.mjs`

Drives the real Player over synthetic terrain (battery is now
11 gates):

- **SD1** stairs down (1.14 → 0.57 → 0): grounded on all 210
  frames, zero landing dip, ends at floor 0.
- **SD2** cliff (3 m): 29 airborne frames, lands with the full
  0.16 m dip — the snap does not glue.
- **SD3** step up (0.57): grounded throughout, stands on top.
