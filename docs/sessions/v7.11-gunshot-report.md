## What the old synthesis was doing wrong — plain terms

The V6-3 layering was right (snap / blast / body / mech / tail
per weapon), but every layer's SHAPE was wrong, so the result
read as a noise-burst sound effect instead of a gun:

1. **No spike at the front.** The "snap" was 12–26 ms of noise
   highpassed at just 1.2–2.1 kHz — a hiss, not a crack. A real
   report leads with a 1–5 ms supersonic transient carrying
   energy well above 5 kHz.
2. **The buffers pre-decayed.** `_noise()` bakes a linear decay
   into every buffer; layered under an exponential gain
   envelope, the attack arrived smeared and the blast spread
   its energy evenly — like TV static — instead of
   front-loading it like a pressure wave.
3. **The body led with a tone.** A pitched sine slide
   (115→48 Hz) reads as a synthetic "bloop"; on a phone speaker
   the lows vanish entirely, leaving only hiss — worst case on
   the owner's actual device.
4. **Nothing ever slammed.** Master gain 0.35, no dynamics:
   every layer summed politely under the cap, so peaks had no
   violence. Real gunfire is dominated by its peak-to-tail
   ratio.

## The new shot (all procedural, zero new nodes per shot class)

- **Layer 0 — THE CRACK (new):** ~5 ms of FLAT (un-decayed)
  noise highpassed per weapon — AR 4.5 kHz, SMG 5.6 kHz,
  shotgun 2.4 kHz (a shotgun's report is blast-led, not
  crack-led), DMR 3.6 kHz at the highest gain, pistol 4.8 kHz —
  at full level from sample zero, through the gunshot bus.
- **Air shear:** the old snap survives, re-voiced quieter
  (~half gain) and brighter (1.6–2.8 kHz) — it's the crack's
  body, not the crack.
- **Blast:** switched to flat noise, so the existing
  exponential gain envelope front-loads the energy; filter
  collapse (5–8 kHz → rumble) unchanged.
- **Body:** sine thump re-voiced DOWN ~25% (support, not lead);
  bandpassed body-noise switched to flat noise and re-voiced
  UP — the audible weight now lives in noise at 150–340 Hz,
  which phone and laptop speakers can actually reproduce.
- **Gunshot bus (new, in ensure()):** own shots route
  gain → DynamicsCompressor (threshold −14 dB, ratio 12,
  attack 1 ms, release 180 ms) → master. Two persistent nodes,
  created once. Peaks can run at real-report level without
  clipping; full-auto glues instead of stacking into
  distortion. Per-shot node count is unchanged (6–7
  short-lived noise buffers, as before).
- **Untouched:** enemyShot (distance model + its snap),
  explosion, hitmarkers, footsteps/foley, UI, music, voice —
  `_burst`/`_tone` gained opt-in `flat`/`dest` parameters that
  default to the exact pre-V7.11 behavior for every existing
  caller.
